Common Problems

Low Borehole Pressure

Water pressure drops and flow becomes unreliable at outlets. This indicates supply instability or restriction in the line. The next step is targeted testing to isolate the affected section.

Pump Not Starting

The pump stays silent and no water reaches the property. The pattern is consistent with pump wear or a pressure-control fault. System testing is used to isolate the failing component before repair work starts.

Pressure Controller Fault

Zones fail to start, skip schedules, or keep running unexpectedly. That condition lines up with wiring faults or unstable controller power. A technician then confirms the fault before the repair scope is agreed.
