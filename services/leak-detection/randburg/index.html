Common Problems

Slab Leak

Warm patches, damp flooring, or unexplained moisture appear inside the property. This indicates hidden water loss below the slab. The next step is targeted testing to isolate the affected section.

Pool Leak

Pool water levels drop faster than normal even when the surface looks calm. The pattern is consistent with a leak around the pool shell or pipework. Pressure and system testing isolate the affected section before repair work starts.

Underground Pipe Leak

Wet ground, soft paving, or unexplained surface moisture appears outside. That condition lines up with damaged pipework below the surface. A technician then confirms the fault before the repair scope is agreed.
