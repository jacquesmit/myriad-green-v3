Common Problems

Low Water Pressure

Sprinkler coverage weakens and zones stop reaching their normal range. This indicates supply instability or restriction in the line. The next step is targeted testing to isolate the affected section.

Broken Sprinkler

A sprinkler head sprays unevenly, leaks, or fails to lift correctly. The pattern is consistent with damaged sprinkler components or uneven pressure. Pressure and system testing isolate the affected section before repair work starts.

Valve Failure

Zones stay on, fail to start, or cycle at the wrong time. That condition lines up with a faulty valve or an electrical issue. A technician then confirms the fault before the repair scope is agreed.
