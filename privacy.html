Introduction

Myriad Green ("we," "us," or "our") operates https://www.myriadgreen.co.za. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

  • Contact forms: name, email, phone, message content
  • Booking requests: name, email, phone, service type, property address, preferred dates, notes
  • Payment information: processed securely through Stripe; we do not store full card details

We also automatically collect limited technical information:

  • IP address, browser type, pages visited
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (via Google Analytics if enabled)

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and process bookings
  • To send confirmation emails and service reports
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To prevent fraud and ensure security

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including encryption, secure API communication, and restricted access to sensitive data. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe: for payment processing (see Stripe's privacy policy)
  • Firebase: for hosting, functions, and database (see Google's privacy policy)
  • Google Analytics (optional): for website analytics

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@myriadgreen.co.za.

Cookies

We use cookies only for essential functionality (theme preference) and optional analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.
